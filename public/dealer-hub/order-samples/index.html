Tips for Ordering Samples

  • Order early in the design process — Give your customer something to hold and compare during the initial consultation. Physical samples close more deals than catalog images.
  • Request the exact finish and door style — Generic samples don't build confidence. Match the sample to the spec you're presenting.
  • Keep a showroom sample library — Maintain a curated set of your most-ordered styles and finishes so you have them on hand for walk-ins.
  • Use samples to upsell — Show the difference between standard and premium finishes side by side. Let the quality speak for itself.
