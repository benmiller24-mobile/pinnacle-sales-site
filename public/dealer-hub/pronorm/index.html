Pronorm German Kitchens — Selling & Ordering Guide

The one-page orientation for selling Pronorm through Pinnacle Sales — the lines at a glance, how design and ordering work, and where every Pronorm resource lives.

The line at a glance

Pronorm is a premium fitted German kitchen brand, manufactured in Vlotho, Germany since 1945 and available in the US exclusively through Pinnacle Sales dealers. It fills the gap between entry-level German imports and ultra-premium brands like SieMatic and Poggenpohl — typical installations run $40,000–$150,000+.

  • Y-Line — the unique handleless kitchen. Integrated aluminum grip profile (stainless, bronze, or black anodized) built into the top of each front; rear handle rail can match or contrast. Pronorm's flagship.
  • X-Line — the classic handleless kitchen. Gently curved C-shaped handle profile in 10 color options for a seamless, unified look.
  • Proline128 — the ergonomic grid kitchen. 128mm planning grid; any cabinet height, floor-to-ceiling configurations; mixes freely with Y-Line and X-Line or push-to-open.
  • 100+ door finishes — high-gloss and premium matt lacquer, anti-fingerprint organic glass, real wood veneers, laminates, ceramic and stone looks, textured melamines.
  • Built for American kitchens — 610mm (24″) base and tall cabinet depths standard, unlike most European brands at 560–580mm.

How selling Pronorm works

  1. Qualify the project. Pronorm wins design-forward, handleless, European-modern projects with real budgets. For contemporary projects below the Pronorm budget, Eclipse is the step-down answer inside your same portfolio.
  2. Design & specification. Pronorm design, catalogs, specification detail, and dealer resources live at PronormUSA.com — that's the system of record for the brand.
  3. Ordering. All Pronorm ordering runs through the PronormUSA process — see the Ordering SOPs page for where Pronorm fits alongside your WW Wood workflow.
  4. Display & showroom. Pronorm display standards and specifications are at PronormUSA.com; for merchandising help in your showroom, schedule a consult.
  5. Support. For design, ordering, and service questions, start at PronormUSA.com or reach your Pinnacle territory rep — the Support & Escalation map has contacts.

Selling notes

  • Lead with 610mm depth. Authentic German engineering that fits American kitchens — no compromise on counters, appliance integration, or storage. It's Pronorm's biggest edge over competing German brands in the US.
  • The three lines combine. Y-Line, X-Line, and Proline128 share one platform — mix handleless fronts with grid planning freely in one kitchen.
  • Sustainability story. Climate-neutral manufacturing since 2021, PEFC certified, "Furniture Made in Germany" mark — material for design-conscious clients.
  • Whole-portfolio play. A Pronorm showroom display anchors your contemporary story; Eclipse and Nevara cover the tiers below it so no modern project leaves your showroom.
