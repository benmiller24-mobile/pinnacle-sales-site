Dealer Onboarding

Your Activation Plan

Let’s get your team comfortable with the line, one step at a time. We usually plan these 12 steps over 6–8 weeks and adjust as we go. Your rep reviews your first order before you submit it and stays involved through three complete kitchens, then confirms when you’re ready to order independently.

Get comfortable with the process

We’ll work alongside you through your first three complete kitchens, from quoting and order review through delivery, installation, and any parts needed to finish the job. Your team prepares and submits the orders; your rep helps you learn the process and checks in along the way. Each kitchen counts as one project, including its related orders. After three complete kitchens, your rep confirms you’re ready to order independently or helps you focus on anything that needs a little more practice.

Support: We’ll get back to you as quickly as we can, within one business day, and let you know who is helping and when to expect the next update. Your team can work directly with the factory on routine status and parts requests. If something is unusual, stuck, urgent, or affecting your customer relationship, bring your rep in right away.
