What's the same across all four

All four brands are built by W.W. Wood Products in Dudley, Missouri (10411 Brenda St., Dudley, MO 63936), ship on the same ~6 week production cycle, and share Pinnacle Sales' support. WW Wood is family-owned, founded in 1977, with more than 1,200 team members on staff, and is one of the top three privately held cabinet manufacturers in the United States.

That means running a job that uses two or more of the four brands gets you one delivery, one acknowledgment, one rep on call, and one warranty process. The dealer portal is the same (my.wwinc.com), Blum soft-close hardware is standard across the lineup, and on Shiloh, Eclipse, and Aspect the catalyzed conversion varnish topcoat is the same.

In practical terms: you can mix Shiloh and Eclipse in the same house — a traditional primary kitchen and a contemporary basement bar — and both lines will ship together on a single acknowledgment.
