Brand story

Eclipse is W.W. Wood Products' frameless full-access answer to the demand for European-style cabinetry at a domestic price point. Officially branded "eclipse by Shiloh," it's built in the same Dudley, Missouri factory as Shiloh, by the same craftspeople, with the same hardware program — but engineered around a contemporary frameless box rather than a face-frame.

Eclipse is American-made frameless, ships in six weeks, and rides on the same WW Wood delivery as a Shiloh order in the same house. It's also the only WW Wood brand that offers the specialty-materials library — high-gloss acrylic, matte velvet acrylic, gloss HPL, matte noir HPL (RAUVISIO), wood-grain HPL, TFL, natural oak/walnut veneers, and a new PV polymer laminate.

On the Metropolitan slab door, Eclipse delivers automatic grain matching across every material — wood, veneer, and laminate — at no upcharge. Grain continuity across a long run of slab fronts is typically a paid custom-order upgrade at this price point, or simply not offered. Eclipse builds it in as standard, and it's one of the strongest things to show a client against any competing frameless line at the Eclipse tier.

Market position: premium semi-custom — the same tier as Shiloh, built on the same WW Wood quality standard. Best fit: a contemporary kitchen where the homeowner wants European look and European materials without European cost or lead time.

Door styles

Eclipse offers the same 50+ door styles as Shiloh (Hanover, Square Flat Panel, Square Raised Panel, Aspen, Heritage, Lancaster, the full Group A and Group B lineup) plus one Eclipse-exclusive: Metropolitan, the modern slab door that's the platform for the specialty materials.

The pricing groups carry over from Shiloh: Group A (no upcharge), Group B (+$44/door). The Metropolitan slab is Group A.

What's special about Metropolitan

Metropolitan is the only door style that can be ordered in the specialty material library — high-gloss acrylic, matte velvet acrylic, gloss HPL, matte noir HPL (RAUVISIO), wood-grain HPL, TFL, and natural oak/walnut veneers. Other door styles run on the standard wood species in paints or stains.

Edge options on Metropolitan in specialty materials: matching, satin aluminum-look, or brushed aluminum-look. Acrylic versions can also be specified with a 3D edge.

GOLA handleless system

Eclipse offers a built-in GOLA channel handle system — an integrated aluminum channel routed directly into the cabinet door or drawer front, eliminating the need for external pulls or knobs. The result is a seamless, ultra-modern aesthetic that's become a signature of high-end European kitchen design, available in Eclipse at a domestic price point.

GOLA channels come in multiple profiles and finishes to match any contemporary project. Pair them with Metropolitan slab fronts and the specialty-materials library for a true Euro-modern kitchen that ships in six weeks.

Finishes & color program

Eclipse pulls from the same finishing operation as Shiloh and adds an entire specialty-materials library that Shiloh doesn't offer. Four categories:

Paints

25 different paint color options plus custom paints from Sherwin-Williams are available. 2025 Trend Colors: Shoji White, Quietude, Rookwood Shutter Green.

Olde World finish also available on the Eclipse paint program. Glazing is paint-program compatible on stains only.

Stains & real-wood

Stains across the same species library as Shiloh — Maple, Alder, Cherry, Red Oak, Hickory, Walnut, Select Poplar, American Poplar, plain/quarter/rift-cut White Oak, and rustic variants. Olde World, Aged distressing, Weathered stains, and the full glazing program are all available on Eclipse — same finishing line as Shiloh. Plus reconstituted veneers: Reconstituted White Oak and Reconstituted Walnut — engineered veneers with consistent grain that designers reach for on long runs.

Stain matching across a Shiloh + Eclipse split kitchen is possible because the finishing line is the same.

Acrylic, HPL & TFL (Metropolitan only)

High-gloss acrylic and matte velvet acrylic — color-matched PMMA top layer with zero-joint edge-banding. Gloss HPL, matte noir HPL (RAUVISIO), and wood-grain HPL (4% deduct each). TFL (Thermally Fused Laminate, 25% deduct) — textured to simulate wood grain, horizontal or vertical orientation.

All of these specialty materials are Metropolitan door style only. RAUVISIO Noir is anti-microbial, ultra-low VOC, phenol-free, and resists scratches and fingerprints.

PV (Polymer Laminate) — NEW

Eclipse's newest material: PV polymer laminate, 10% deduct off base. A durable, contemporary surface that drops price compared to acrylic or HPL while staying in the modern aesthetic. Like the other laminates, available on Metropolitan only.

When to specify Eclipse

The simple decision rule:

Reach for Eclipse when:

  • The client wants true full overlay — clean, continuous fronts with no visible face frame between doors and drawers (only frameless construction delivers this)
  • The aesthetic is contemporary, modern, or Euro-leaning — slab, narrow shaker, textured
  • The client wants frameless full-access storage but isn't ready for European pricing or lead times
  • The project mixes a primary kitchen and a basement bar / butler's pantry / ADU and you want continuity at a lower price tier
  • The showroom needs a modern story to anchor the contemporary side of the floor — pair with the Eclipse Merchandiser Display
  • The client appreciates the n/c matching between the door front and the tops/bottoms of Eclipse wall cabinets — finished to match the door rather than left as a raw edge
  • You want PV or laminate finishes in a US-made line

Reach for a different Pinnacle brand when:

  • The project is traditional, transitional, or needs inset / beaded inset — Shiloh fits better
  • The job is value-driven with a tight lead — Aspect serves that price point
  • The client wants frameless but the budget won't reach Eclipse — Nevara sits roughly 50% below on a 3/4" plywood box
