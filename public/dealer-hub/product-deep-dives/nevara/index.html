Brand story

Nevara is the newest cabinet line from W.W. Wood Products in Dudley, Missouri — the same family-owned factory that builds Shiloh, Eclipse, and Aspect. Where Shiloh's story is breadth and Eclipse's is specialty materials, Nevara's story is focus: a tightly curated selection of SKUs, finishes, and door styles, engineered so dealers can quote faster, order with fewer decisions, and deliver consistent results at a price point that competes with in-stock and flat-pack cabinets.

The line was designed to sit about 50% below Eclipse while keeping the construction story intact: frameless full-access format, a full 3/4" plywood box, dovetail drawers, and Blum soft-close hardware — fully assembled in Missouri. As the line is newer, spec books, catalogs, and order forms are available through your Pinnacle rep and the WW Wood dealer portal.

Door & finish program

Nevara is intentionally the opposite of Shiloh's 50-style library. The program centers on the Westbridge door — a 5-piece Shaker profile in painted white MDF — with no color-customization program. That constraint is the feature: one decision instead of forty, a predictable finished product, and a price that holds.

What's in the program

Westbridge painted white 5-piece MDF Shaker door, full 3/4" plywood frameless boxes, dovetail drawers, Blum soft-close hardware, and the standard WW Wood spec-book sizing.

What's not

No stain program, no custom or multi-color paint program, no specialty materials, no inset. If the client's design turns on finish or color, step up to Aspect or Shiloh; for specialty frameless fronts, Eclipse.

Why it works

White Shaker remains the volume request in the territory. Nevara answers it with real plywood construction and soft-close performance at a price that competes with in-stock cabinets — while keeping the order inside the WW Wood portal, acknowledgment, and support process you already use.

When to specify Nevara

The simple decision rule:

Reach for Nevara when:

  • The client is upgrading from in-stock or flat-pack cabinets and wants real plywood construction without a semi-custom budget
  • A builder needs to hit a price point but wants WW Wood reliability, one portal, and a predictable ~5–6 week lead
  • The design is white Shaker, frameless — and speed and consistency matter more than customization
  • You're quoting against a home-center stock line and want to win on construction quality at a comparable number
  • The project is volume work — rentals, spec homes, ADUs, multi-unit — where a repeatable single-SKU program is an advantage

Reach for a different Pinnacle brand when:

  • The finish or color is the design driver — Shiloh (widest program) or Aspect (value framed, stains available)
  • The client wants specialty frameless fronts — acrylic, HPL, TFL, PV, GOLA handleless — Eclipse is the answer
  • The design calls for inset or face-frame construction — Shiloh or Aspect
