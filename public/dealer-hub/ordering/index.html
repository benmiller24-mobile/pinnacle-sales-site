Ordering Standard Operating Procedures

Step-by-step process from project quote to delivery for every Pinnacle Sales brand.

Get comfortable with the process

We’ll work alongside you through your first three complete kitchens, from quoting and order review through delivery, installation, and any parts needed to finish the job. Your team prepares and submits the orders; your rep helps you learn the process and checks in along the way. Each kitchen counts as one project, including its related orders. After three complete kitchens, your rep confirms you’re ready to order independently or helps you focus on anything that needs a little more practice.

Support: We’ll get back to you as quickly as we can, within one business day, and let you know who is helping and when to expect the next update. Your team can work directly with the factory on routine status and parts requests. If something is unusual, stuck, urgent, or affecting your customer relationship, bring your rep in right away.

  5. Review your first orders together — During your first three complete kitchens, send your order packet to your rep before you submit it. Work through any changes together and keep the review with the job. Your team prepares the forms and submits the orders; your rep coaches and reviews. After three complete kitchens, your rep confirms readiness to order independently.
  6. Submit Order to WW Wood — Once the client approves, submit the order through the WW Wood online ordering portal. Include PO number, ship-to address, and any special instructions.
  7. Order Acknowledgment Review — WW Wood sends an order acknowledgment confirming specs, pricing, and estimated ship date. Check it as soon as it arrives and note the factory’s current correction deadline. Mark corrections directly on the acknowledgment and resend to orders@wwinc.com. Keep the correction open until the revised acknowledgment shows the agreed change. Ask the factory to confirm any effect on price or schedule.
  8. Production & Tracking — Your latest acknowledgment and factory updates are the best starting point. The ship week, warehouse arrival, home delivery and installation each have their own timing, so confirm the next step before setting a date with your customer. Track your order status through the WW Wood portal, check your delivery acknowledgment, or email customer.service@wwinc.com with your order number for a delivery ETA.

    Understanding Lead Times for Standard Orders from WW Wood

  9. Delivery Inspection — Inspect all cabinets upon delivery. Note any damage on the carrier's bill of lading before signing. Report damage or missing items promptly; confirm and meet the applicable current factory/carrier reporting deadline. Contact Pinnacle Sales if you need help with a claim.
