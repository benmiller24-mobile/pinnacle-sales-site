Product Deep-Dive

Construction methods, finish options, pricing structure, lead times, and key differentiators for every brand in the Pinnacle Sales portfolio.

Shiloh Cabinetry

Construction

Shiloh is a framed (face-frame) cabinet line built on ¾″ plywood construction. The face frame provides additional structural rigidity and a traditional aesthetic that resonates with the majority of American homeowners. Shiloh offers both standard overlay and six distinct inset door configurations — more inset options than virtually any competitor at this price point.

Finish Program

Shiloh offers an exceptionally deep finish program including stains, paints, glazes, distressing, and specialty techniques across multiple wood species: Maple, Cherry, Hickory, Quartersawn White Oak, Rustic Alder, and more. The custom paint program matches any Sherwin-Williams color — a major differentiator when designers need a specific color for their project.

Key Selling Points

  • Broadest inset offering at this price tier (6 inset configurations)
  • Custom paint matching to any Sherwin-Williams color
  • Full ¾″ plywood box construction standard
  • Soft-close hinges and drawer slides standard on all cabinets
  • 10-year warranty
  • Lead times typically 4-6 weeks depending on configuration

Eclipse Cabinetry

Construction

Eclipse is a full-access frameless cabinet line with ¾″ plywood box construction. Frameless construction means no face frame — the door covers the entire cabinet front, creating the clean, modern European aesthetic increasingly demanded by today's homeowners and designers. Eclipse is built on the same production lines as Shiloh, delivering premium frameless quality at an accessible price point.

Standout Features

  • Vertical grain matching included at no charge on all wood veneers and wood grain laminates
  • Tops and bottoms of wall cabinets automatically match the door finish at no upcharge with standard box construction
  • Full-access interior — wider drawers and more usable storage than face-frame equivalents
  • Same finish program as Shiloh, including custom Sherwin-Williams paint matching
  • 10-year warranty

Framed vs. Frameless — How to Present It

Many customers don't understand the difference. The simplest explanation: framed cabinets (Shiloh) have a wood border around the cabinet opening, giving a traditional look with visible frame between doors. Frameless cabinets (Eclipse) have doors that cover nearly the full cabinet face, creating a sleek, contemporary appearance with minimal gaps. Both use the same ¾″ plywood — the choice is aesthetic, not quality.

Standard Cabinet Modifications

Eclipse supports a wide range of standard modifications — height changes, width adjustments, depth alterations, and more — that let you tailor cabinets to fit nearly any project requirement without a custom quote. Watch the walkthrough below to learn what's available, how to specify modifications correctly, and which changes are considered standard vs. custom.

GOLA Channel Collection

Eclipse offers a built-in GOLA channel handle system — an integrated aluminum channel routed directly into the cabinet door or drawer front, eliminating the need for external hardware. The result is a seamless, ultra-modern aesthetic that's become a signature of high-end European kitchen design. GOLA channels are available in multiple profiles and finishes to match any contemporary project.

Aspect Cabinetry

Construction

Aspect is the value-tier offering in the WW Wood Products portfolio. It provides solid cabinetry with select finish and door style options at a more accessible price point — ideal for rental properties, budget-conscious renovations, or entry-level new construction.

Key Details

  • Streamlined door and finish selection for faster ordering
  • 5-year warranty
  • Shorter lead times than Shiloh/Eclipse
  • Strong option when budget is the primary driver

Nevara Cabinets

Overview

Nevara is the newest W.W. Wood Products line — value-tier frameless cabinetry on a full 3/4" plywood box, fully assembled in Dudley, Missouri. The program is deliberately curated: a painted white Westbridge 5-piece MDF Shaker door ships standard, with no color-customization program. Designed to sit roughly 50% below Eclipse while keeping dovetail drawers, Blum soft-close hardware, and edge-banded panels. ~5–6 week lead time on your existing Shiloh/Eclipse multipliers. Full reference: Nevara deep dive.

Pronorm German Kitchens

Overview

Pronorm is a premium fitted German kitchen brand manufactured in Vlotho, Germany since 1945. Available in the US exclusively through Pinnacle Sales dealers. Three product lines (Y-Line, X-Line, Proline128) can be freely combined on a universal platform. Over 100 door finishes, 610mm (24″) American-depth base and tall cabinets, and construction details that set it apart from both domestic and imported competitors.

Key Differentiators

  • 610mm (24″) base and tall cabinet depths — fits American kitchens without compromise
  • ProTech X premium drawer system: 60kg soft-close capacity, no visible screws
  • Organic glass doors: anti-fingerprint, scratch-resistant, hygienic
  • Climate-neutral since 2021, PEFC certified, "Furniture Made in Germany" mark
  • Fills the gap between entry-level German brands and ultra-premium (SieMatic, Poggenpohl)
  • Typical installations: $40,000–$150,000+

Vistora Closets

Overview

Vistora is a fully assembled modular closet system built by W.W. Wood Products in Dudley, Missouri. Where typical closet systems require 20-30 individual parts per closet, Vistora uses 4-5 pre-assembled floor-mounted modules with accessories already built in. Nine finish options. Hardware in Matte Black and Matte Chrome. Ideal add-on for kitchen dealers who want to capture whole-home projects.
