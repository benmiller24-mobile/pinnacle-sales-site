Dealer Onboarding Checklist

Your detailed onboarding reference guide. Follow this week-by-week roadmap to ensure your launch goes smoothly.

Get comfortable with the process

We’ll work alongside you through your first three complete kitchens, from quoting and order review through delivery, installation, and any parts needed to finish the job. Your team prepares and submits the orders; your rep helps you learn the process and checks in along the way. Each kitchen counts as one project, including its related orders. After three complete kitchens, your rep confirms you’re ready to order independently or helps you focus on anything that needs a little more practice.

Support: We’ll get back to you as quickly as we can, within one business day, and let you know who is helping and when to expect the next update. Your team can work directly with the factory on routine status and parts requests. If something is unusual, stuck, urgent, or affecting your customer relationship, bring your rep in right away.

Expected Onboarding Timeline

Week 1 Sample Order & Acknowledgment
Week 2 Portal Activation
Week 3 In-Person Product Training
Week 4 Live 2020 Webinar
Week 5–6 First Orders (with review)
Week 8+ Independent Ordering

Timelines are approximate and depend on scheduling availability. Most dealers are fully ramped within 6–8 weeks.

Step 1 — Place Initial Sample Order Week 1

  • ✓
    Request your starter kit
    Email your Pinnacle Sales territory rep with your sample-order request for approval and processing. Your starter kit includes door samples and color chips at a 50% showroom discount. We also recommend the full chip set with a sample of every color in chip format (SE Deck Complete). Download the correct form from Order Samples.

Step 2 — Door Sample Order Entered Week 1

  • ✓
    Review your acknowledgment
    WW Wood Customer Service will enter your sample order. You'll receive an acknowledgment email — review your door-sample and color-chip order promptly and follow the current factory correction deadline. Mark any corrections on the acknowledgment and resend to orders@wwinc.com.

Step 3 — Dealer Portal Activation Week 2

  • ✓
    Activate your WW Wood portal account
    The dealer portal lets you place, track, and inquire about orders. You'll receive an activation email from our team once your dealer number is issued. Activate immediately upon receipt. See Ordering SOPs for the full order workflow.

Step 4 — Onboarding Training Session 1 (In-Person) Week 3

  • ✓
    Product & sales orientation (~90 min)
    Your territory manager will schedule an in-person session at your showroom. Covers cabinet construction and SKUs, finish overview, quoting tips, and competitive positioning. Target: within 10 days of receiving your samples. Review materials ahead of time on the Product Deep-Dive page.

Step 5 — Onboarding Training Session 2 (Live 2020 Webinar) Week 4

  • ✓
    Hands-on 2020 Design catalog walkthrough (~90 min)
    Live webinar covering WW Wood 2020 Design catalogs: catalog setup, spec rules, pricing, and order-form export. Our Dealer Success & 2020 Specialist will reach out to schedule. Target: 1 week after your in-person session. Browse tutorials and tips on the 2020 Software Training page.

Step 6 — Placing Your First Orders Weeks 5–6

  • ✓
    Submit your first order for review
    We’ll work alongside you through your first three complete kitchens, from quoting and order review through delivery, installation, and any parts needed to finish the job. Your team prepares and submits the orders; your rep helps you learn the process and checks in along the way. Each kitchen counts as one project, including its related orders. After three complete kitchens, your rep confirms you’re ready to order independently or helps you focus on anything that needs a little more practice. Follow the step-by-step process on the Ordering SOPs page.
