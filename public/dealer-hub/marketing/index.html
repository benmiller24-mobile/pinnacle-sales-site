Posting Cadence

  • Frequency: 2–4 posts per week per platform. Quality over quantity — one strong install photo beats five filler shots.
  • Best times: Tuesday–Thursday, late morning (10am–noon local) or early evening (6–8pm local). LinkedIn skews mornings; Instagram skews evenings.
  • Mix: Aim for ~60% installed-project photos, ~20% process / behind-the-scenes (showroom, install crews, design sessions), ~20% educational (door styles, finish stories, brand differentiators).
  • Tag your suppliers: Tag @PinnacleSales, the brand (e.g. @ShilohCabinetry), and your installer / designer. Cross-tagging multiplies reach without extra effort.
  • Story / Reel mix: Stories work for in-progress and showroom shots; Reels work for finished walkthroughs. Both feed the main grid algorithm.
