Order Management

Order Status & Tracking (WW Wood)

Check production status, estimated ship dates, and delivery tracking for Shiloh, Eclipse, Aspect, and Nevara orders. Your latest acknowledgment and factory updates are the best starting point. The ship week, warehouse arrival, home delivery and installation each have their own timing, so confirm the next step before setting a date with your customer.

First: Check the WW Wood online portal or your delivery acknowledgment
Email: customer.service@wwinc.com with your order number for a delivery ETA
Escalation: Bring your rep in if something is unusual, stuck, urgent, or affecting your customer relationship. Share who you’ve spoken with and the latest update

Order Changes & Modifications

Review the acknowledgment immediately and follow the correction deadline in the applicable current factory instructions. Mark changes directly on the acknowledgment and resend to orders@wwinc.com. Always confirm you receive a revised acknowledgment before considering the order final.

Acknowledgment corrections: orders@wwinc.com
Also notify: Your reviewer during your first three kitchens, or your rep if you need extra help
Note: Ask the factory to confirm any effect on price or schedule

Warranty & Service

Damage Claims (Shiloh / Eclipse / Aspect)

Shipping damage, manufacturing defects, or missing items. Report promptly with photos and follow the applicable current factory/carrier deadline. Complete the brand-specific warranty form and submit with your claim.

First contact: WW Wood Customer Service — 573-624-7090
What to provide: Completed warranty form, order number, photos of damage, description of issue
Escalation: Bring your rep in for anything disputed, unusual, or still unresolved. Call right away if it’s urgent or affecting your customer relationship

Warranty Claims (Post-Installation)

Finish issues, hardware failures, or structural defects discovered after installation. Use the applicable manufacturer warranty, including eligibility, conditions, and exclusions. See current guidance and sources.

Step 1: Fill out the brand-specific warranty claim form
Step 2: Submit to WW Wood Customer Service — 573-624-7090
What to include: Completed warranty form, original order number, install date, and detailed photos
Escalation: Your Pinnacle Sales territory rep (see team table below)
